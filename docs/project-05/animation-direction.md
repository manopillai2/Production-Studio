GOLDEN RULES
- Mira sings; Mango reacts. They NEVER do the same move at the same moment: when one acts, the other listens, watches, tilts its head, wags, sniffs or nods with a different timing (stagger by at least 0.5 s).
- Every shot: both characters have their own actions timeline (2-4 actions per shot each, spread across the shot), and look at each other (look_at / turn_to) whenever they are not facing the viewer.
- Keep them at least 1.2 m apart; paths never cross each other or the props. Walk/run across the frame (x) or toward the camera, never away from it. Feet stay on the ground (y = 0); only the kite and the balloons go up.
- Face the camera (rot_y 0) when singing to the viewer.
- Camera rhythm: open each place with a wide shot, then full and medium shots, close-ups on key words (a close-up of Mango for his lines, of Mira on "copy"). Never the same shot size three times in a row. Repeat the same camera pattern in every chorus.
- Lighting: garden = morning (lines 1-16), meadow = day (lines 17-24), meadow = sunset (lines 25-36).
- Transitions: dissolve into every chorus, iris when the place changes (line 17), cut otherwise.
